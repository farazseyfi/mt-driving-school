BC's graduated licensing system

From learner to full licence.

L

Learner stage

You practice with a fully licensed supervisor while building fundamentals — this is where lessons with your instructor fit in.

N

Novice / Class 7

After passing the road test, you drive independently under novice conditions while continuing to build experience.

Full

Full licence

Once novice requirements are met, you move to a full, unrestricted driving licence.

Getting ready

General checklist for your road test day.

  • Valid learner licence documentation
  • A roadworthy vehicle with valid insurance and registration
  • A fully licensed supervisor if required for your stage
  • Comfort with mirrors, signals, and basic vehicle controls
  • Practice with the specific manoeuvres ICBC tests on
  • A calm, well-rested start to the day
